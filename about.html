About COHO Analytics

Free Colorado housing data and tools for people who plan, build, fund and run affordable housing: need by town and county, rental markets, and where tax credits and state housing money have gone.

Platform Purpose

COHO puts Census, HUD, CHFA and DOLA figures for every Colorado county and town on one site, with the source named on each page. It shows what each community needs, what the rental market looks like, which deals have been funded, and what a new deal would take to pencil.

It is built for developers, lenders and investors, housing authorities and nonprofits, and town and county staff.

Who Built COHO

COHO Analytics was developed and is maintained by pggLLC, a Boulder, Colorado planning, strategy and housing development firm founded in 2013 and incorporated in 2015.

pggLLC also works on affordable housing projects in Colorado. COHO's rankings, scores and reports apply the same method to every county, town and place, including places where pggLLC works.

Why LIHTC Matters

The Low-Income Housing Tax Credit program is the federal government's primary tool for encouraging private investment in affordable rental housing, and has been since Congress created it in 1986. In Colorado, CHFA's records list 926 tax-credit projects through 2025.

While federally authorized, LIHTC operates through a state-level allocation system. Each state housing finance agency designs its own Qualified Allocation Plan (QAP), creating distinct development environments, risk profiles, and investment outcomes across regions.
