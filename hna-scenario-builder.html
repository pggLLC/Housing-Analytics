Projection Scenario Builder

See how many homes a place needs over 20 years if births, deaths or migration differ from the State Demography Office's forecast. Every setting starts at that forecast, so with nothing changed you get the HNA page's 20-year figure.

Screening tool only. Projections are planning estimates from public data: the State Demography Office, the National Center for Health Statistics and the Census Bureau. They are not certified forecasts and do not substitute for a formal housing needs study or CHFA-required analysis.

How homes are counted: homes needed = households added since 2024 ÷ (1 − the county's target vacancy), as on the HNA page. Households = households per person in four age groups (SDO's forecast, or 2024's held) × the scenario's people in each group. Methodology →
ℹ Model assumptions & provenance
Base population
SDO county forecast, Vintage 2024: people by single year of age and sex in 2024
Births and deaths
Colorado's rates by age (NCHS: state life tables, 2021; births, 2023), scaled each year so each county's totals equal SDO's forecast
Migration by age
SDO's forecast: the change in its population by age that births and deaths do not explain. Or the same residual from SDO's estimates for the ten years before the forecast, averaged and carried forward each year (Smith, Tayman and Swanson, 2013)
Households
SDO's household forecast by age of householder (18-24, 25-44, 45-64, 65 and over). Or its 2024 households per person in each group, held the same every year: the constant headship rate method (Smith, Tayman and Swanson, 2013)
Vacancy target
Each county's target vacancy, as on the HNA page
Towns and CDPs
The HNA page's share of county growth: from the place's share of county households and of 2020–2024 building permits (Census ACS and building permits); a few places without one use their share of county population. Or a share of each age group: the place's and the county's people by age and sex in the 2010 and 2020 Census (table P12), each age group's ten-year change carried forward (Hamilton and Perry, 1962; Smith, Tayman and Swanson, 2013)
Shaded range
How far past forecasts missed: trend forecasts of every Colorado county from SDO's population estimates, 1970–2024, launched each year from 1980 and checked against SDO's later estimate. The range holds 2 in 3 of them, for counties of the place's size and that many years out (Smith, Tayman and Swanson, A Practitioner's Guide to State and Local Population Projections, 2013)
Model type
Modeled: cohort-component projection by single year of age; outputs are scenarios, not certified forecasts
