Rent context — voucher vs. existing vs. asking

Three rent measurements for the active county. HUD FMR is a voucher benchmark, not a LIHTC rent limit. LIHTC gross rent limits come from HUD multifamily income limits (MTSP), as published by CHFA, using 1.5 persons per bedroom (one person for an efficiency). Contract rent deducts the utility allowance and required fees. Compare that net limit with supported market rents to assess achievable rent.

Soft-Funding Program Reference (Colorado)

Gap sources sit alongside LIHTC equity and the first mortgage: CHFA's gap and preservation loans, Proposition 123 funds, Division of Housing grants and loans (including HOME, the National Housing Trust Fund and CDBG), city housing funds, historic and new markets tax credits, and seller notes. The Funding Sources tab of the Colorado Deep Dive lists every Colorado program with its award size, rate, term, AMI restriction and next round quoted from the official source, and its finder narrows them to your deal type and county. The breakdown below the Sources & Uses table above lists only programs whose current terms were confirmed on the program's own page. To apply for tax credits with state or Denver gap money, the agencies share one workbook, CHFA's 2027 Common Application, and one calendar on Housing Hub Colorado.
