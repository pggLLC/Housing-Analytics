Colorado Preservation Inventory

Existing affordable rental properties listed on the Colorado Housing and Finance Authority (CHFA) preservation map. CHFA lists properties that receive or have received an incentive to keep them affordable, such as rent supplements, below-market financing or Housing Tax Credits, drawn from CHFA's portfolio and from the Colorado Division of Housing, the City and County of Denver, USDA and HUD. Copy of the CHFA map layer retrieved September 2026. CHFA updates its own portfolio annually and partner data at staggered intervals. The map layer lists each property's name, address, city, ZIP code and total units. It does not list subsidy type or expiration dates; verify current affordability restrictions with CHFA.

Each property is placed in a county from its mapped location, using simplified census county boundaries, so a property close to a county line can land in the neighboring county. CHFA reports that, as of December 2024, affordability restrictions on nearly 21,000 rental units in Colorado are set to expire in the next 15 years (CHFA preservation). The property list does not show which properties those are.

A property missing from this list may still carry affordability restrictions; the map covers properties CHFA and its partners report.
