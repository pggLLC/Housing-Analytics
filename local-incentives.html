How to read this page

Each record was read from the jurisdiction's own code, ordinance, fee schedule, ballot result or program page, and the wording it rests on is quoted under it. A figure the source does not publish is left blank rather than estimated.

A jurisdiction with no record has not been shown to have no program. Every jurisdiction is marked verified records, checked, none found, source could not be read, or not yet checked.

Data Sources & Review Rules

  • Fee relief and land-use incentives: data/policy/fee-reductions.json. Local funds, taxes, linkage fees and ownership tools: data/policy/local-housing-funds.json. Who was checked and found nothing: data/policy/incentive-coverage.json. Tool catalog: data/policy/incentive-alternatives.json.
  • Prop 123 status: data/policy/prop123_jurisdictions.json, from DOLA's commitment filings. DOLA's page blocks automated reads, so the list is refreshed by hand; its date is shown beside every status.
  • Every record carries a re-check date. A daily job opens an issue before a record falls due, and the record shows a warning here once it is overdue. A monthly job opens an issue naming the next jurisdictions to research.
  • State and federal programs are on the Colorado Deep Dive; property-tax abatements are in the tax-abatement inventory.
