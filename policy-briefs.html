    About this feed

    What it is. Headlines about Colorado affordable housing and the federal policy that shapes Colorado deals (HUD, Congress, Treasury, LIHTC trade press), pulled from public news feeds and refreshed weekly. Stories that are only about other states are left out.

    What it is not. Editorial reporting. Headlines and publisher names are the publishers' own, and COHO does not rewrite them. The same story from several outlets is shown once, with the other outlets listed under it.

    Places and local numbers. A headline that names a Colorado city, town, CDP or county is tagged with that place and its region, the same ones the Housing Needs Assessment uses, and shows one line of that place's own numbers with their survey vintage. A number the data does not have is left out, not shown as zero. The line describes the place, not the project in the story.

    Policy watch is curated by hand, not pulled from a feed. Each entry says how it was checked: against the primary document, with the exact wording it rests on kept in the data, or as reported by a named outlet. Topics nobody has checked yet are listed as not covered, rather than filled in from search results.

    Before you quote it. Open the original article. If a link is blocked, "Alt. link" searches Google News for the same headline.

    Research briefs marked source-reviewed have been checked against the sources they link. They are analytical summaries, not substitutes for the original research.
