          Sources and limits
          • Scoring, thresholds, credit caps and gap funds: CHFA 2027-28 QAP Third Draft (posted 11 Sep 2026). CHFA had not adopted it when this was checked; every figure here is "not adopted" until it is.
          • Prop 123 status: DOLA's commitment filings (cdola.colorado.gov/commitment-filings). 2027-2029 filings are due 1 Nov 2026.
          • Housing need points and the lowest-AMI county list come from CHFA's application materials, not the QAP. Enter them only from CHFA's list.
          • A score is not an award. QAP Section 5: "Regardless of numerical ranking, the scoring does not operate to vest in an Applicant or project any right or reservation or allocation of Housing Tax Credits in any amount."
          • Files you add are not uploaded or kept. Only the figures you type, the file name and a fingerprint are saved.
          • Who else may apply in Round One: Next round. Something unclear? Ask pggLLC.
