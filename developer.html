Start here

Three steps take you from a town's name to a first call:

  1. Pick a place. Type a town or county below, or choose one from the Pipeline or Where to build.
  2. Read its place card. The top of the report says how ready the place is, who to call first and why, and which sites are open.
  3. Log the talk, or ask pggLLC. Note what was said in Discussions on the card. If anything is unclear, ask pggLLC.
How a conversation moves through the pipeline
  • Screen — passed the Opportunity Finder filter; partnership conversation not yet started.
  • Signal — a recent partnership-relevant moment is documented (RFP, joint conversation, public-land discussion, or funding window).
  • Outreach — you're in active conversation with the community.
  • IC — internal review of the opportunity is underway.
  • Active — working together on a defined project.
  • On Pause — moved to the anti-targets list; explicitly set aside to revisit later.

The pipeline, signal log, anti-targets and network live in your private files; the gated site reads them. The full methodology is on the Pipeline page.
